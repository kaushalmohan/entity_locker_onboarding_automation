Entity Locker Onboarding Automation

How onboarding businesses onto EntityLocker can be fully or partially automated — using India's digital public infrastructure.

Onboarding Requirements

To onboard any business, we need to answer two questions

1

Proof of Establishment

"Is this a real entity?"

2

Proof of Authority

"Is this person allowed to act on behalf of the entity?"

To automate onboarding, both these questions need to be answered instantly.
